## Problem

Impacts land with no weight. The specified procedural effects, hit-stop, and shake are projected by the core but never drawn by the app. The game's biggest moments (a Camera kill, Network Blackout, a boss phase) have no payoff.

## Proposed outcome

- **Every impact:** the specified effects, hit-stop, and shake render.
- **Big moments:** each has a distinct, bounded payoff.
- **Accessibility:** everything respects Reduced Motion and Reduced Flash.

## Affected users/systems

- **Player:** presentation and audio.
- **Specification:** `procedural-vfx-002`, `animation.md` § 8a, `audio-haptics.md`, D-088.
- **Runtime:** VFX rendering, hit-stop, shake, and the Blackout music drop.

## Constraints/non-goals

- Presentation only: no digest, receipt, or rules change.
- Hit-stop caps are 50/90 ms. No white flash. No combat zoom.
- Fits the iPhone 12 budget (to be measured under T606).

## Open questions

- None.

## Verified claims

- `VFXProjector` exists and is tested in core. No `App/` code consumes it.

## Assumed claims

- Hit-stop implemented as a presentation freeze keeps the simulation's fixed step and determinism intact.
